Rationale, continued

because it says relationship without a heart or a chat bubble, and it says Thera without a postcard: the coin is the island, the caldera and the Θ, the vase is Akrotiri pottery, and the profiles are the classical statue cut down to a brow, a straight nose and a chin. What the judges said

  • brand strategist The only mark in the set a founder can explain in one breath and that then rewards a second look: two people face to face, and the space they share is a Greek vase on the coin of Thera. Two cautions: the Rubin vase next to the name 'thera' can read as a therapy or psychology brand, so smooth the lumpy lip and chin bumps and soften the nose tips so the calm vessel reading leads; and the 6-unit neck is the fragile part at 20 px, so widen it to 7 or 8 units and re-check the 16 px render.

Rationale, continued

Because it is two solid masses on a pixel-aligned cut it is the most legible mark in the exploration at 16 px, works in a single colour on any background, and sits on the baseline next to "thera." like a letter. Its honest weakness is that split discs have cousins in fintech and design-tool marks and the mark says place rather than person; the slide, the horizon of light and the dome-over-reflection accent are what make it thera.'s, and the warmth is carried by the wordmark and the voice, which is where this product's warmth lives anyway.

What the judges said

  • brand strategist The cleanest Stripe/Linear-era gesture here and the best favicon of the eight: dome over horizon, reflection in the caldera, theta with a crossbar of light, in one sentence. Its weakness is strategic: shifted half-discs are a crowded family in fintech and design-tool marks, and it says place with no person or relationship in it, so the wordmark has to carry all the warmth. Keep the offset (it is what separates this from theta-caldera), try the lower half in muted blue to borrow theta-caldera's sunset reading, and trim the lockup viewBox, which is still 320 wide with the content in the left 45% unlike every other concept.
  • mark craftsperson The strongest geometry in the set: one r21 circle, one 8-unit cut, one 12-unit slide, point-symmetric about (32,32), no feature smaller than 8 units, and the horizon gap lands on whole pixels so the 16 px favicon is two crisp half-discs. The defects are outside the mark: the lockup keeps a 320-wide viewBox with about 140 units of dead space right of the period (trim to ~196), and the split-and-slid disc has cousins among fintech marks. Test a 10-unit slide so the two chords overlap more and the form reads as one sliced object rather than two shapes at 48 px; Mark A's arch-and-dot is a weaker, top-left-heavy composition with a softer ring at 16.
  • product and UI practitioner Variant b is the sharpest favicon in the set: two solid half-discs whose chord edges land on whole pixels, a clean 2 px horizon gap at 16 px, and nothing lost in a single light colour on #1C2E3D. Rationale, continued

  • brand strategist Variant B's two-tone disc with a slot is calm, warm and instantly legible, and the period in thera. echoing the lower blue segment is the best lockup detail in the set.
