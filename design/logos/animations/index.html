From the island and the goddess to the mark.

Two transformations drawn in the brand's own colours and geometry: a goddess simplifies into the "Goddess on the coin" mark, and Santorini simplifies into the "Caldera and Sunken Head" mark. Both scrub in either direction, so the logo can also blossom back into the picture.

Earlier warm versions (v1)

The first round started from fully rendered warm illustrations and only reached the brand colours near the end. They are kept here for comparison and do not play on their own.

Each animation is a self-contained SVG module with a single set(t) function, so it can be dropped into the website hero or the app's launch screen. Playback respects reduced-motion settings: with that preference on, nothing plays by itself and the slider still works.
